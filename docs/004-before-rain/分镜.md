# 《雨停之前》｜最终分镜

6组镜头，每镜5秒；16:9；Pika H3 Flex FL2VA；首尾帧应先完成一致性验收。

固定女主：Lin Xia, 21-year-old East Asian university student, neat jaw-length straight black bob, SILVER CRESCENT hairclip on the LEFT, pale-blue knit cardigan over ivory blouse, charcoal pleated skirt, slim dark shoulder bag, and a DARK NAVY hardcover notebook
固定男主：Chen Yu, 22-year-old East Asian university student, short softly wavy black hair, FOREST-GREEN varsity jacket over white crew-neck T-shirt, dark trousers, ONE transparent umbrella with a curved white handle

## 分镜列表

## 镜头 001｜笔记本落地

场景摄影：Rainy university stone arcade; fixed medium-wide, ivy-covered pillar left, wet courtyard right

第一帧：Lin waits at LEFT pillar looking down at her closed navy notebook on the wet ground. Chen enters far RIGHT under his ONE OPEN clear umbrella

最后一帧：Same frame: Chen has walked closer and bends to pick up the same navy notebook with his free hand. Lin stays at the pillar, watching. Umbrella remains OPEN

5秒连续运动：Chen steps nearer, bends and retrieves the notebook; Lin lifts her gaze. Tiny slow camera push-in

## 镜头 002｜同一把伞

场景摄影：Same stone arcade and window as 001, fixed 50mm medium two-shot, Lin LEFT and Chen RIGHT

第一帧：Chen extends the navy notebook toward Lin; Lin extends her hand. Chen's OPEN transparent umbrella covers only Chen

最后一帧：Same frame: Lin hugs the notebook to her chest, steps half a pace closer, and the same OPEN clear umbrella is now centered above BOTH; two small smiles

5秒连续运动：Lin receives the notebook while Chen slowly tips the canopy to shelter them both, followed by restrained eye contact

## 镜头 003｜伞偏向她

场景摄影：Same rainy tree-lined campus path with wet stones and lampposts; slow 50mm side tracking, Lin LEFT Chen RIGHT

第一帧：Both walk under one OPEN clear umbrella angled toward Lin; Chen's outer right shoulder is wet. He holds handle in RIGHT hand; Lin holds navy notebook in LEFT arm

最后一帧：Same tracking perspective: Lin's RIGHT hand touches Chen's on umbrella shaft, nudging canopy toward the middle. Their shoulders are sheltered evenly; notebook still in LEFT arm

5秒连续运动：They take two slow steps. Lin notices his damp shoulder and gently centers the umbrella by touching the shaft; subtle side tracking

## 镜头 004｜图书馆空位

场景摄影：Warm university library, SAME pale-oak table, rainy window, amber reading lamp; static 35mm medium-wide shot

第一帧：Lin sits LEFT at table, navy notebook in front of her; Chen stands RIGHT behind an EMPTY oak chair. The SINGLE clear umbrella is CLOSED and leans on RIGHT table leg

最后一帧：Same camera and room: Chen now sits in the RIGHT chair facing Lin, both smiling faintly; notebook and CLOSED umbrella remain in EXACTLY same positions

5秒连续运动：Chen slides out chair and sits; Lin offers a small inviting gesture; camera holds still

## 镜头 005｜纸上小伞

场景摄影：EXACT SAME library oak table and rainy window as 004; 50mm seated two-shot including hands and faces

第一帧：Chen on RIGHT draws a simple umbrella pictogram on an off-white note with black pencil; Lin watches from LEFT. Navy notebook stays closed by her left hand. No words

最后一帧：Same camera: Lin's RIGHT hand holds note with the SAME single umbrella pictogram above the notebook, she smiles; Chen sets pencil down. No added objects or text

5秒连续运动：Chen completes pencil line, sets pencil down, slides note; Lin lifts it and smiles naturally

## 镜头 006｜雨停以后

场景摄影：Blue-hour path outside university library, rain STOPPED, wet reflective stones, amber lamps; static rear three-quarter 35mm medium-wide shot

第一帧：Lin walks LEFT, navy notebook in LEFT arm. Chen walks RIGHT, CLOSED clear umbrella in RIGHT hand. Their adjacent hands—her RIGHT and his LEFT—are separated

最后一帧：Same perspective and path: Lin's RIGHT hand and Chen's LEFT hand have interlaced, both walking slightly farther from camera. Notebook and CLOSED umbrella unchanged

5秒连续运动：They walk two slow steps; Lin reaches across with right hand, Chen joins with left, slight camera pull-back, no cut
